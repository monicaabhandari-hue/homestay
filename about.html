About the Hosts

Babin & Geeta
Your Mountain Family

At Saipatri Homestay, we welcome every guest like family.

Nestled in the peaceful hills of Kalimpong, our home is a place to slow down, unwind, and experience genuine hospitality. We take pride in offering a cozy and comfortable stay where every guest feels at home in the hills.

One of the most special parts of staying with us is our fresh, home-cooked meals, prepared with care using locally available ingredients. We want every guest to experience not just a place to sleep, but a true taste of life in the mountains.

Our aim is simple — to make your stay comfortable, memorable, and truly special.
