The Saipatri Way

Experience the Stay

Nestled at 1,250 metres above sea level in Echhey, Kalimpong, Saipatri Homestay offers a rare glimpse into slow mountain life — where mornings begin with mist rolling in from the valleys and evenings settle into birdsong and distant prayer bells.

Stay with Babin and Geeta, your warm and welcoming hosts, who take deep pride in making every guest feel truly at home. From locally sourced Himalayan meals to unhurried conversations over chai on the veranda, every moment here is an invitation to breathe deeply and simply be.

Local Experiences

01

Fresh Flowers

Geeta grows and sells a beautiful selection of fresh flowers from the homestay garden — marigolds, seasonal blooms, and more. A piece of the hills to take home or offer at a nearby temple.

Prices upon request
02

Home-Cooked Meals

Savour freshly prepared Nepali and Kalimpong hill cuisine — steaming momos, thukpa, gundruk, and seasonal vegetables sourced from the local market and our own kitchen garden.

03

Village Walks

Meet artisans, farmers, and keepers of centuries-old stories rooted in the Kalimpong hills. Explore monasteries, vibrant local markets, and the rich living heritage of the region at an unhurried pace.

04

Sunrise Viewpoints

Watch the first light paint Kanchenjunga's snow-clad peaks in shades of gold and rose on clear Himalayan mornings. Our exclusive viewpoints offer unobstructed panoramas that stay with you long after you leave.
